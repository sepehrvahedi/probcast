COMP0105 Financial Institutions and Markets · Group coursework · AAPL

Many ideas.
One honest forecast.

ProbCast is a point-in-time, probabilistic forecasting lab for any listed stock, built first for Apple. Each idea becomes a probability forecast that is backtested using only what was public at the time, scored against a CAPM anchor, and combined with everyone else's into one cited 12-month view.

What did we know, and when?

00-overview/project-charter.md

Three promises

Nothing from the future

Every number knows when it became public

Every row carries an available_at timestamp. An analysis run "as of" a date can only see what was published by that day's close: SEC filings by acceptance time, macro data by release vintage, prices rebuilt so later stock splits don't leak backwards.

Tested, not just promised: a leak-test suite runs on every analysis. It rewrites all data after the forecast date and checks the answer doesn't change.

Honest scores

Every idea is graded, including the ones that fail

Ideas produce probability distributions, not buy or sell calls. Each one is scored against a CAPM anchor with confidence intervals, an effective sample size, an A–D evidence grade and a count of every variant tried. Apple's survivor-biased past drift can't sneak in: the calibration is drift-neutral and tested.

Scores whose outcomes fall in 2021 or later stay sealed until one witnessed reveal on 1 Dec. We call it pseudo-out-of-sample, because we all know Apple's recent history.

One forecast, with receipts

The team's ideas combine into one cited view

A committee gives each idea family one equal vote, holds the CAPM anchor at a fixed 25%, and produces the 12-month fan chart with an exact breakdown of who moved the median.

Proof: every figure ships with a caption, a source line and a Harvard reference a teammate has opened.

06-architecture/analyzer-contract.md

How an idea becomes evidence

Idea card

A teammate writes a hypothesis, the data it needs and a success test. The card is timestamped before any testing.

Analyzer

the coding agent implements it as a plugin that can only read ctx, the as-of view of the data.

Walk-forward test

Month-end forecasts from 1990 onward at 1 week, 1, 3, 6 and 12 months, each a 23-quantile distribution.

Score

Skill vs the CAPM anchor, calibration, n_eff, grade A–D and a trials count. "Insufficient evidence" is a valid verdict.

Committee

One vote per idea family plus the anchor. The committee is backtested like any analyzer.

Report evidence

Fan chart, scenarios at ±15%, ratio and peer tables, figures with captions, references and a numbers ledger.

Research rules we agree to

  1. Pre-register before testing. An idea is tested only after its card is registered.
  2. Don't use ProbCast results for 2021 onward to design ideas. Those scores are sealed until 1 Dec.
  3. Every variant counts. The trials total appears in the report.
  4. Negative results are results. "Does not beat the CAPM anchor" is a finding.
  5. ProbCast never writes prose. It supplies numbers, figures and citations.
  6. We cite only what we opened.
  7. No advice language. The prediction is a distribution, not a recommendation.

08-roadmap/risk-register.md

Top risks and how they're handled

16
Schedule overrunThe whole of Release 1 is about 1.5–2× the build capacity before 25 Nov. Mitigated by building the engine first, two interface drops, parallel agents, checkpoints and an agreed cut order.
15
A survivor-biased headlineApple's own past drift (about 18% a year) would inflate the forecast because we picked a known winner. Forecasts centre on CAPM, calibration is drift-neutral with a gating test, and the report shows how the forecast moves under each anchor choice.
12
Usage limits or the product owner being awayParallel agents can hit plan limits, and the product owner can fall ill. A reduced-lane mode is agreed in advance, and the evidence floor (Fri 20) and forecast floor (Mon 23) land early, so the editor can write from the team folder.
12
Overclaiming from little dataThere are only about 45 independent 12-month windows. Every skill number shows a confidence interval, an effective sample size and a grade. 12-month claims are limited to calibration.
12
Hindsight in the implementerThe coding agent knows Apple's history, so it may not pick or tune parameters. It uses the card's values or literature defaults, and every variant is logged.
12
The product owner as bottleneckTeammates own sections and the editing. Idea cards arrive in capped waves and are processed in two batches a day.
Planning process

How this plan was made

Seven research agents explored the product, the quant methods, the data, Apple's history, the analysis library, the interface and the architecture. Two critics attacked their work, a synthesiser merged it, and two writers produced the specifications. An independent red team then reviewed the final plan and signed it off as "ready with fixes"; all 40 of its findings were addressed in v1.1. Every brief and output is kept.
