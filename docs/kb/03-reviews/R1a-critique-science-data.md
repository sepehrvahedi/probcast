Severity: **Critical** (blocks R0/R1 or changes the report's headline number), **High** (grade, credibility or PIT integrity at risk), **Medium** (fix before R1 freeze), **Low** (tidy-up).

**Overall view.** The ideation is unusually strong on point-in-time (PIT) design. A2's bitemporal model, future-poisoning test, honest-power table and CAPM anchor are better than most professional backtesting specifications. A4's 88 EDGAR-timestamped earnings rows held up in every row checked. The problems are of a different kind:

1. The five documents disagree on the core contract.
2. One hidden modelling choice (the drift anchor) decides the headline forecast.
3. Several concrete data-pipeline defects would break the "PIT-verified" claim on day one.
4. The science scope is several times what can ship by Wed 25 Nov.
5. The design is optimised for forecasting machinery. The brief mostly marks descriptive financial analysis.
